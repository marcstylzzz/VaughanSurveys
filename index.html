About the Firm

Built on Trust and Canadian Soil.

Vaughan Land Surveyors is an established Ontario surveying firm (under Provision Land Consultant Inc.) deeply connected to the natural landscapes we map. Based in Colborne, we provide critical foundational data for property owners, developers, and municipalities.

Our work is authoritative. When you stand on land we've surveyed, you stand with confidence. We combine decades of local historical knowledge with cutting-edge measurement technology to define, protect, and document property boundaries across Northumberland County.

Our Expertise

Professional Surveying Services

Delivering precise, legally-defensible land data for residential, commercial, and municipal clients across Ontario.

Boundary Surveys

Authoritative identification and marking of property lines. Crucial for resolving disputes, erecting fences, and understanding exact ownership limits.

Topographic Surveys

Detailed mapping of natural and man-made features, including elevations and contours. The essential foundation for architectural and engineering design.

Construction Layout

Translating engineering blueprints into precise ground markings. We ensure buildings, roads, and utilities are built exactly where designed.

Subdivision Planning

Comprehensive surveying for dividing larger tracts of land into smaller lots. We handle the complex geometry and municipal requirements.

Legal Descriptions

Drafting precise, legally-binding text descriptions of land parcels for deeds, easements, and official municipal records.

SRPR Surveys

Surveyor's Real Property Reports providing detailed documentation of structures, encroachments, and easements on a property — essential for real estate transactions in Ontario.

Our Methodology

Accuracy Demands Process.

01

Historical Research

Before stepping on site, we analyze historical deeds, old survey plans, and municipal records to establish the theoretical framework.

02

Field Execution

Our crews deploy advanced robotic total stations and GPS/GNSS technology to gather highly accurate spatial data on your property.

03

Boundary Resolution

We reconcile field measurements with historical documentation, applying surveying law to determine true legal boundaries.

04

Final Deliverables

You receive authoritative, stamped plans and documents ready for legal, architectural, or municipal use.
